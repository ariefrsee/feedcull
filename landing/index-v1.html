Why this exists

When thousands of readers asked platforms for a way to filter AI-generated articles, the answer was a policy debate. So readers started building their own extensions and killfiles — the demand was already there. FeedCull is that idea, done properly: a curation layer that lives in your browser, not in a platform's moderation queue.

Why it is NOT an "AI detector": detection is unreliable, and false accusations hurt real people. FeedCull never says "this is AI." It shows you pattern signals and lets you decide — and it ships with the filters that are 100% reliable: your killfiles, your topic rules.
