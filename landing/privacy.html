Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-04 · Version 1.0 · One page, plain language, no fine print.

FeedCull is built on a simple premise: a feed curator that watches you is a contradiction in terms. This policy is the contract that enforces that premise.

1. The extension

FeedCull is a browser extension that hides content you don't want to see on Hacker News and Reddit. Everything it does — reading headlines, matching your killfiles and topic filters, running the heuristic scorer, hiding rows — happens locally in your browser. The extension has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no telemetry.

2. What the extension stores

  • Your settings (killfiles, topic filters, sensitivity, per-site toggles) are stored in your browser's chrome.storage.sync. If you have Chrome sync enabled, Chrome may mirror that storage through your own Google account as part of Chrome's normal sync behaviour — that is Google's infrastructure and governed by Google's own privacy terms. FeedCull never receives it.
  • A daily counter (posts culled today) is stored in chrome.storage.local on your machine only.
  • Nothing else. No browsing history, no page content, no keystrokes, no cookies, no network requests.

3. The optional Pro LLM-judge (phase 2)

Pro's article-text scoring will send the text of an article you explicitly ask to be scored to a language-model provider. This will be an explicit, per-request action — never automatic, never background — and the text is not stored by us. The free tier has no such feature at all.

4. This website

  • Waitlist form: when you join the waitlist, your email address and a timestamp are sent to our list service and saved. That's the whole dataset — no name, no location, no tracking. It's used to notify you about FeedCull and nothing else. It is never sold.
  • Fonts: this site loads its typeface from Google Fonts. Your browser requests that font from Google, so Google sees your IP address per its own privacy policy — standard for the web. No font request is made by the extension itself.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no ads.

5. Children

FeedCull is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the version and date above will be updated. Because FeedCull collects almost nothing, meaningful changes are unlikely — and if one ever happens, it will be announced, not buried.

7. Contact

Questions, concerns, or deletion requests: open an issue on GitHub.
