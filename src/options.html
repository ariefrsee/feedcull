FeedCull — Options

Your rules, stored locally in your browser. Nothing leaves your machine in v1.

Global sensitivity

Flagged posts are hidden but the count is shown; culled posts are removed from view. You can always change this.

Killfile — domains

One domain per line. Every post from these domains is culled, everywhere.

Killfile — authors / usernames

One username per line (exact match).

Topic filters

One keyword per line. Posts whose title contains the keyword are culled. The "hide AI-related articles" crowd asked for exactly this.
